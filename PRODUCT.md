# Distortion 0.1.4

Native JUCE residual analyzer for music mastering. One instance is the final master insert; its external mono/stereo sidechain carries the unprocessed source. Main output is bit-identical, with zero added latency. No activation, preferences or plugin-owned bypass.

The fixed 560 × 236 window shows Short, Integrated and Reset, with a “Music estimate” descriptor. There are no graphs or spectrum calculations. Short uses three seconds of accepted sample energies and updates every 100 ms after model learning. Integrated is the ratio of summed residual and output energies since reset, excluding warm-up and unavailable data; Reset preserves Short. Pauses make Short unavailable; pauses and capture-epoch changes preserve Integrated. Unknown/unavailable measurements use an em dash.

The live model compensates reference gain, delay, finite FIR/EQ, phase and fixed channel mixing, then fits a separate affine gain trajectory per channel in 20 ms segments. An exact two-level gain-jump detector handles an identifiable abrupt level change. This reduces level/envelope contributions, but cannot separate all compressors or transient shapers from distortion. Fast dynamics, noise and finite-model error can remain; fitting can also suppress some nonlinear energy, especially at low frequencies. Percentages estimate residual RMS relative to output RMS, not true THD on music or a live test tone. The CLI retains its harmonic THD_F test-tone method. There is no loudness weighting, loudness gating or certification claim.

Identity follows Clip Pocket Solid Dark and embedded Inter, with pale blue percentage readouts on a dark recessed panel. The editor polls at 10 Hz using a nonblocking snapshot attempt. Model learning waits at least two seconds after each completed fit before another fit, reducing background CPU use. FFT convolution remains necessary for linear prediction; spectral display processing is removed. Windows VBlank pacing fix, font licensing, UTF-8 configuration, float/double support and process/UI separation are retained where relevant.

Release targets: Windows x64 VST3/CLAP, macOS Universal VST3/AU/CLAP, AAX Developer. Local Linux verification is not evidence of release-platform validation. Source upload remains with the user; only the build workflow is written directly to their GitHub repository.
